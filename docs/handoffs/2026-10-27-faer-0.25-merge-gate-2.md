## Decision rule (declared before the runs, unchanged afterwards)

Per-cell statistic: the median of the three process-set medians (a start is one process set), for each revision;
a cell's ratio is candidate / baseline of those. Family ratio: the median of that ratio over the family's cells.

* Gate 1 (procedure): `scripts/candidate_compare.py` control, the unchanged `faer-upstream` median ratio within
  2%; otherwise no merge.
* Gate 2 (adoption, declared by the coordinator): no merge if any family's median candidate/baseline ratio is
  above 1.03, or if any cell is *robustly slower by more than 10%*: all three candidate starts slower than all
  three baseline starts and the cell's median ratio above 1.10. Cells robustly slower by 5-10% (same all-three-
  starts condition) do not block; they are listed as known residuals in `PATCHSET.md` and here.
* A refused comparison is not re-run with a relaxed rule or a trimmed start. If either gate refuses, the result
  is written below and nothing is merged, published or tagged.
* If both pass: fast-forward `main` to `6cb3949`, confirm tree hashes (`candidate_compare --merged-commit`),
  record `PATCHSET.md` and the worklog, move `pins/t4a-faer.rev`, publish `t4a-faer` 0.25.0
  (`t4a-faer-traits` stays 0.24.0), tag `faer-v0.25.0`, merge harness PR #27 when its own checks pass.
