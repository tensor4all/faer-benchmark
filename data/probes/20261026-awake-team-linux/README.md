# Awake-team acceptance run on Linux (tensor4all-meta#51), 2026-10-26

The acceptance measurement of the awake-team design (threadteam teams carried by the placement token, `Par<'team>`
in the faer fork, dispatch-cost thresholds; tensor4all-meta#48) on a quiet Linux host, including the x86_64 product
path that the M5 run could not exercise. Tables: [`tables.md`](tables.md) (generated by `summarize.py all`). Raw data
and the per-process host record: `raw/`.

## Host, protocol and revisions

* Host `evo`: GMKtec EVO-X1Pro, AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 470, 12 cores / 24 CPUs (SMT2, sibling of `n` is `n+12`), one
  NUMA node, two L3 domains {0-3,12-15} and {4-11,16-23}; Linux 7.0.0-38-generic; governor `performance`, EPP
  `performance`, **boost off** (`cpufreq/boost = 0`, set through sysfs before the run, every process recorded `0`),
  so every core runs at 2.0 GHz. Absolute times and serial rates are therefore those of a 2.0 GHz core, not of the
  host's default boosted state.
* Pinning: measured process 1T `taskset -c 4`, 4T `4-7`, 8T `4-11`; observer (the agent, its shell, the runner) on
  `0-3,12-15`, pinned with `taskset -apc` on the agent's process tree before the first process.
* Protocol: tensor4all-agent-rules `rules/benchmark/measurement.md` at `786aae2` and faer-benchmark
  `docs/protocol-v2-baseline.md` (timing v2: prime 250 ms, median, row rotations) at `4b6b99e`, applied by
  [`hostguard.py`](hostguard.py): idle guard on the whole measured L3 domain before and after every process (3 s
  window, max busy 0.05, 8 attempts, 30 s wait), refusal while any cargo/rustc/test/benchmark process runs or boost
  is on, the unmeasured CPUs of the domain sampled every 10 s during the run (reject above 0.20), thread variables
  removed from the measured environment. **101 processes, all accepted at the first attempt**, none busy, the
  largest during-run sample of an unmeasured CPU of the domain 0.006 (`raw/processes.log`, last table of
  `tables.md`). 11:53 to 13:22 JST.
* Revisions (all clean): threadteam-rs `d08b3e7` (code identical to `5afe0b4`, which the fork and the downstream
  crates pin; the difference is docs only), faer `2107b81` (`exp/par-team-lifetime`), strided-rs `6821b53`,
  tcontract-rs `597ef0f4`, cpueinsum-rs `38360c8`, tlinalg-rs `cf6fa84`, upstream faer `0539947` (v0.24.4). Harness:
  faer-benchmark `ad8014d` (`bench/awake-team-linux`, from `4b6b99e`); the binaries were built from the same tree
  before it was committed. rustc 1.99.0. Binary sha256 in `raw/build-info.txt`. fbench `--features faer-only,team`
  (no vendor arm).

## Acceptance conditions: recommendation

| # | condition | result on Linux x86_64 | recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `Par<'team>` carries team authority | builds; threadteam 139/156 tests (default/all features) and loom pass, faer 370 with `threadteam` pass. **On x86_64 the authority leaks**: `linalg/matmul/internal/mod.rs:172` (`spicy_matmul`) and `linalg/matmul/triangular.rs:~648` call `private_gemm_x86::gemm(.., par.degree())`, and `degree()` of a team is its size, so that work runs on spindle threads outside the team. `diag-gemm` shows it: Cholesky 512/1024 under the team row, 8 busy threads outside the team, 7 to 9 s of CPU outside it per 24 s; LU and QR show none | **reject on x86_64 until fixed**; the design itself is not refuted (the leak is two call sites that have no `Par::Team` arm) |
| 2 | serial thresholds derived from the token's dispatch cost | every gate measured follows `estimate >= PAYS * sections * D` exactly (strided, faer GEMM tiles; arithmetic below). But on Linux `host_default()` is the generic table (team 3, in pool 15, outside 40 us), while the measured costs are team 2.8/2.2/1.7 us, in pool 3.1/3.6/3.5, outside 9.5/10.1/8.8 at widths 2/4/6. The width model above 6 is 2 times pessimistic (width 8 measured 1.11 us on a team, model 2.21). The serial rates in faer/strided are the M5's; the 2.0 GHz Linux rates are 1.4 to 3.7 times larger | **accept the rule, reject the Linux constants**: add a measured Linux row, anchor the width model at the largest measured width instead of growing it in proportion, and decide whether rates are per host class |
| 3 | members do not spin beyond the idle spin when no section runs | confirmed: after construction, after the last section and after one isolated section the members spin for 200 ms and then park (traces T4 and T8: 120 ms + 80 ms of spin, then 90 percent parked; `idle-trace-*.txt`) | **accept** |
| 4 | the fork's process-wide parallelism setting removed | not implemented (planned in `docs/par-team-migration.md`) | **not met**; outside this run |
| 5 | quiet Linux run including the x86_64 product path | done. Team GEMM on x86 goes through `utils::team::gemm_tiled` (the `gemm` crate per member), never `private-gemm-x86`: square 512 to 2048 the team row is 1.05 to 1.11 times the held row (a kernel difference, not dispatch). Cholesky under the team is 1.1 to 5.8 times slower (the leak of row 1). LU wins (0.65 to 0.86), QR is mixed (0.62 to 1.06), SVD/EVD equal | **reject the x86 product path as is**; accept after the leak is fixed and the team tiles call the x86 kernel |

## Findings

### Dispatch cost (threadteam, condition 2)

Microseconds per section, 10 us shares, back to back, pool of 8 (median of 3 processes; `tables.md`):

| kind | w=2 | w=4 | w=6 | w=8 | M5 (w=2/4/6) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| team | 2.8 | 2.2 | 1.7 | 1.1 | 3.0 / 2.3 / 1.4 |
| plain in pool | 3.1 | 3.6 | 3.5 | 3.0 | 6.0 / 11 / 5.5 |
| plain outside | 9.5 | 10.1 | 8.8 | 6.7 | 14 / 18 / 19 |

The team is as cheap as on the M5; a plain token is about half the M5's cost. Penalties not in the table (the design
keeps them out): a team after its members parked costs 82 to 84 us (M5 27), a plain token from outside after a 10 ms
gap 103 to 120 us; the first share starts 63 to 83 us after a park (`wake_probe`). Linux parks and wakes are about 3
times the M5's.

### Gates under the generic table (condition 2)

* strided (`strided-basic/src/cost.rs:153`, `is_parallel` at width 2): copy, map, `exp` and `sum` switch together
  at 131072 elements on a team, 524288 in the pool, 2097152 outside: `3 * D(2) / rate` with the generic `D(2)` and
  0.100 ns for copy, map and `exp`. Two class errors: a contiguous `copy_into` is gated as `Map`
  (`kernel.rs:740`), and an opaque `map_into` closure such as `exp` is always `Map` (1.38 ns `Transcendental` is
  reachable only from `strided-fused`). `exp` at 65536 elements stays serial for 360 us on a team (the gate counts
  6.6 us). Once switched, the team wins up to 8 times (0.12 at 524288).
* faer team GEMM (`utils/team.rs:283`, cost at the full width): 64^3 is 9.7 us at the faer rate 0.037; it pays at
  width 4 (`3 * 3.0`) and is refused at width 8 (`3 * 3.0 * 8/6 = 12`), so `gemm64` in the 8 thread sequence is
  1.10 times serial while the 4 thread one is 0.32. With the measured row, width 8 would be `3 * 1.1 = 3.3` us.
* faer `Par::Rayon` rows use the generic `Outside` cost: `gemm` 128^3 (134 us serial) stays serial on the held, hot
  and free rows (0.134 ms at 4 and 8 threads), where the team row takes 0.020 ms at 8 threads.
* tcontract and the cpueinsum chain switch at `n = 64` on a team (0.32 and 0.35 of serial) and at 192 on a plain
  token; tlinalg batched LU/QR switch at `n128xb8` on every token, and the team is 1.1 times the plain token there.

### Serial rates (1 thread, core 4, 2.0 GHz)

`tables.md`, "Serial throughput constants". GEMM 0.064 ns per MAC f64 (M5 0.037), 0.257 c64, 0.032 f32, 0.128 c32;
the factorization steps are 1.4 to 3.7 times the M5's. The triangular inverse rate is size dependent here too: 0.047
ns per `m^3` at the smallest shape against a median of 0.019 (range 0.014 to 0.047), the same shape as on the M5
(0.022 at 64 against 0.0096), so the open item "size dependent rate for the triangular inverse" is confirmed on
Linux. The team-vs-serial triangular inverse at 128 itself was not timed in this run.

### Stage comparison (condition 5), team row over held row

8 threads (4 threads in brackets where it differs in sign): GEMM 128^3 0.15 (0.29), 256^3 0.63 (0.95), 512^3 to 2048^3
1.08 to 1.11 (1.05 to 1.06), 64x4096x64 0.36 (0.26), 4096x64x64 1.02 (1.10), 2048x2048x64 1.17 (1.12); LU 128 1.00,
256 to 2048 0.65 to 0.77 (0.73 to 0.86); QR 128 1.06 (0.99), 256 0.62 (0.78), 512 to 2048 0.96 to 1.04; Cholesky 128
1.44 (1.19), 512 4.21 (1.37), 1024 2.95 (1.43), 2048 5.75 (2.26); SVD values and EVD 0.99 to 1.07. Small-operation
sequences at 8 threads: chain 0.25, `gemm128` 0.18, `gemm64` 1.10 (gate above), LU 64/128 1.00. Team CPU per wall in
the sequences is 1.0 where nothing opens, so with a settle of 300 ms the parked team costs nothing between
sequences.

QR 128 (the PAYS margin item): the team is 1.06 times the held row at 8 threads and 0.99 at 4. The gap is within a
grid step of the rule; this run does not support changing `PAYS = 3` (a cost table measured on this host would move
the QR gates more than the multiple does).

### Harness

* The M5 runner rotated rows by reordering `--rows`, which `fbench run` ignores: its "rotations" all ran the same
  order. This run uses `--rotate-rows` (4 positions per cell; the `pos` column is the spread of the team ratio over
  positions, at most 1.25 except Cholesky under the leak, 3.5 to 10.7).
* `scripts/build_for_faer_rev.sh` refuses git worktrees; the fbench build used clean clones in
  `awake-team-linux/extern-build/` at the same revisions.
* The downstream lock files pin the fork at `9cd64f0`; the condition 1 tests of strided, tcontract, cpueinsum and
  tlinalg ran against that, the `stack` probe here patches the fork to `2107b81`.

### Condition 1 test failures on Linux

* tcontract `batch_claiming.rs:176`, `faer_schedule.rs:65`, `workspace_bounds.rs:319` and cpueinsum
  `prepared_semantics.rs:249` fail on this host: each expects a pool entry that the generic table refuses. The tests
  depend on the M5 table through `host_default()`; they should set their costs explicitly.
* threadteam `--no-default-features`: the doctest `PoolBuilder::dispatch_costs` (`src/pool.rs:406`) asserts a width
  2 cost on a pool of width 1.
